About Moto Tap

MotoTap Autosystems connects drivers with trusted mechanics, garages, and parts dealers across Kenya.

Our shared goal

A thriving automotive ecosystem: map matching, a trusted service network, and growth for independent garages.

For drivers

Search a service, see nearby providers, then call or chat. You do not need an account to find help.
